Why AESI?

We build confidence, discipline, and academic excellence.

AESI exists to restore academic excellence among students and campus workers. We believe diligence in academics and spiritual growth can coexist, and we create the structure that makes that possible.

  • Personalized mentorship and accountability
  • Focused academic development strategies
  • Safe, encouraging community support
